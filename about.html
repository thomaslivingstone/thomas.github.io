Thomas Livingstone

Chef & Food Enthusiast

Hi there!

I am a passionate industry-taught chef currently based in Winnipeg, Manitoba. My career began like many others, still in high school a friend helped me get a job washing dishes and since then I haven't left the kitchen. I worked my way from dishwasher to cook to leadership roles. This progression has provided me a special opportunity to understand the kitchen at all levels and has taught me what builds a successful team.

I've had the opportunity to work in many excellent kitchens with excellent people to learn from. I've been able to learn all day-to-day operations of the restaurant, menu and recipe development, training and team building and inventory management. I enjoy the challenge that hospitality work brings each day.

For me cooking is more than just following recipes and cooking dishes. I take pride in understanding techniques, ingredients and the process of bringing an experience together.

I'm always looking to learn more, whether its creating a new recipe, working a dinner service or even doing paperwork I look at every experience as an opportunity to improve myself.

This website is the culmination of my culinary experience, projects and recipes.
